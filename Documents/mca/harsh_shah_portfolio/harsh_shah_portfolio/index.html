A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Where teaching meets technology.

My work sits at the intersection of education, software development, and student career support.

I’m an Assistant Professor and Web Developer with a Master of Computer Applications background and a strong interest in practical technology.

In the classroom, I help BCA and MCA students understand programming and web development through concepts, examples, and hands-on practice. Outside the classroom, I explore application development and AI/ML while helping students discover internships and employment opportunities through placement coordination.

Through CodeByHarsh, I also aim to make programming topics easier to understand with accessible, learner-friendly educational content.
